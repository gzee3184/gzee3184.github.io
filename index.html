Senku: a world model with a global latent and state tokens

2026 · world models · open source

A self-supervised world model that plans from pixels. Each frame becomes a global latent plus 49 spatial state tokens, trained on frames and actions only. With the same planner as LeWM: 88.8% vs 74.2% on OGBench Cube, 37.3% vs 9.0% on starts that need a grasp, and a tie on PushT. It is worse at placing a held cube away (11.0% vs 57.0%).

LongHorizonWM: subgoal chaining for long-horizon latent planning

2026 · world models · planning

A CEM planner works when the goal is a few steps away and fails when it is far. I trained a chainer and a reachability head on a frozen JEPA encoder to turn a distant goal into one the planner can reach. On PushT with the goal 100 steps out, success goes from 12.3% to 74.1%.

Agentic 5G fault diagnosis with a 1.5B model1st place

2026 · AT&T AI Telco Troubleshooting Challenge, Track 3 · MWC Barcelona

Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct runs as an orchestrator over two tools: a data analyzer that turns raw logs into structured telemetry, and an XGBoost classifier that gives it a first guess. We also fine-tuned the model, but the prompted version scored higher (76% vs 71% on test), so we submitted that. With Vivian Tsang, Wei Ye, and Zhi-Li Zhang.

Predicting career outcomes and salaries of college graduates

2025 · data mining · group project

We used the National Survey of College Graduates, 1993 to 2013: 461k rows and 357 features, most of them sparse. I built and evaluated the salary models. XGBoost does best at 72.9% accuracy, ahead of gradient boosting, CatBoost, a decision tree, and kNN.
